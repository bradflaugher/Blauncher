- One search bar for everything: type to find an app, or press Go to search the web.
- Home and your apps are one screen: tap the search bar to open your apps, swipe down or press Back to close them.
- Your apps open on their categories. Tap one to see its apps, the ones you use most first.
- The key button is now a shortcut you can point at any app, with 33 icons to choose from.
- Swipe up, left or right to open an app you pick in Settings > Gestures.
