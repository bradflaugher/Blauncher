- Home and your apps are one surface: tap the search bar for your app categories under it, or type to find any app.
- Tap a category to see its apps. Emphasis and folded rows are gone.
- Swipe up, left and right each open an app you pick; the first swipe asks which.
- The key button opens any app, with about thirty glyphs to choose from.
- The search bar keeps its size until your text needs another line.
